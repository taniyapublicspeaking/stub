BIBLE EXPLORER

Biblical Prophets

Explore people identified in Scripture as prophets or prophetesses, with descriptions and passages for further reading.

“Major” and “Minor” are traditional groupings based mainly on the length of the prophetic books, not on a ranking of spiritual importance. This catalogue distinguishes named biblical prophets from broader prophetic figures; some identifications and dating are debated.
